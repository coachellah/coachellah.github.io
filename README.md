# Coachellah — your gym companion

A tiny personal gym coach that lives in your browser. No account, no downloads, no internet needed after it loads. Everything you log stays on **your** phone.

**Open the app: https://coachellah.github.io/**

## How to use it

1. Open the link above on your phone.
2. Answer the coach's interview (2 minutes — name, goals, your trainer situation).
3. Use it at the gym:
   - **Log a trainer session** (if you have a PT) right after your session — write down what you did and what you learned. Every cue you save is coaching you never have to pay for twice.
   - **My learnings** — your personal training manual. Add anything: machine seat settings, form cues, how something felt.
   - **Solo workouts** — two simple full-body workouts (A and B) for days you train on your own. Big buttons, rest timer included.
4. When you want coaching feedback, tap **Export** and send the text to the Coach. You'll get back an adjusted plan.

## Good to know

- Your data lives in your phone's browser storage. If you switch phones or browsers, your history doesn't follow — so pick one device and stick with it.
- Add it to your home screen (browser menu → "Add to Home Screen") and it feels like a real app.
- This is a hobby project in its earliest days. If something feels clunky or impersonal, say so — that's exactly the feedback that shapes it.
